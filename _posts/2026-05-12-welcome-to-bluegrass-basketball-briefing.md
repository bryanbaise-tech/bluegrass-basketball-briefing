Welcome to the **Bluegrass Basketball Briefing**, your go-to source for daily Kentucky Wildcats basketball news, analysis, and updates.

## What to Expect

This blog is your automated daily digest covering everything Big Blue Nation cares about:

- **Game recaps** after every UK basketball game
- **Recruiting news** and commitment updates
- **Transfer portal** activity
- **Practice and injury reports**
- **Season previews and outlooks**

## About This Blog

Posts are generated automatically and pushed straight to this site through a GitHub Actions pipeline. Every day, fresh content lands in `_posts/` and is built and deployed by Jekyll to GitHub Pages, no manual intervention needed.

## Stay Tuned

Bookmark this page, subscribe to the RSS feed, and check back daily for the latest from Lexington. Go Big Blue!

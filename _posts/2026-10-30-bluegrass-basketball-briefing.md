## Cats Notebook

### Diallo under the microscope

Zoom Diallo's exhibition production has become the dominant storyline exiting the preseason, and the beat is converging on a single read: the staff is unconcerned, the fanbase is not. The Washington transfer finished sixth on the team in scoring at 6.0 PPG on 38/25/20 shooting splits while leading the roster in turnovers at 2.7 per game, prompting him to [deactivate his Instagram account](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-is-not-concerned-about-zoom-diallo-or-the-noise-surrounding-his-play/) after the UCF finale. [Mark Pope expressed zero concern](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-isnt-worried-about-zoom-diallo-im-telling-you-hes-gonna-have-big-moments-for-us/) about Diallo's adjustment to a system that requires him to move the ball faster than he did at Washington, where he was allowed to pound it as the focal point. Diallo still finished the preseason tied for the team lead with 14 assists, though [Pope kept him on the bench in key second-half moments](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185966/zoom-diallo-struggled-exhibition-season-mark-pope-is-keeping-the-faith) against both Big 12 opponents.

### Mason Williams forces the rotation question

The counterweight to the Diallo discourse is the freshman who was signed in part as a package deal with his father. [Mason Williams averaged 14.0 PPG on 69.6 percent shooting and 66.7 percent from three](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-is-not-concerned-about-zoom-diallo-or-the-noise-surrounding-his-play/) across the three exhibitions against Little Rock, Texas Tech and Central Florida. His [box score plus-minus of plus-42](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186062/mason-williams-incredible-preseason-starter-uk) led the team through the preseason slate, and the freshman has drawn starter-level consideration from the beat heading into Tuesday's opener against Manhattan.

### Injury report

[Kam Williams will not play Tuesday against Manhattan](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186146/kam-williams-injury-return-manhattan), with Pope moving the junior wing from week-to-week to day-to-day following a Jones fracture setback. Pope said the staff is "operating under such extreme caution" and will require "every light to be green" before clearing him, though he confirmed Williams has moved into live scout-team work. [Pope expects Williams to provide a boost without roster adjustment](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kam-williams-is-not-expected-to-play-in-season-opener-for-kentucky/) once cleared, framing it as a plug-in addition rather than a rotation overhaul.

### Preseason verdict and outside reads

The Wildcats finished exhibition play 3-0, closing with 23 assists against 12 turnovers on 32 made field goals in the overtime win over UCF. [Johnny Dawkins called Kentucky a "terrific team"](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-a-terrific-team-with-a-lot-of-potential-says-johnny-dawkins/) after his UCF squad took the Cats to overtime, predicting Pope's third roster "will become a great team" by season's end. Preseason SEC honors landed with the frontcourt: [Milan Momcilovic was named second team and Malachi Moreno third team](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-news/186133/malachi-moreno-milan-momcilovic-earn-preseason-sec-honors) by the league's coaches, with Momcilovic scoring at least 15 in all three exhibitions and Moreno posting a near triple-double (16/14/8) against UCF.

### 2027 recruiting board shrinks

Kentucky went 0-for-3 on 2027 targets who announced this week. [DeMarcus Henry, the No. 2 overall prospect and top-ranked small forward, committed to North Carolina](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185895/demarcus-henry-commits-north-carolina-tar-heels-uk-recruiting) over Kentucky, Arkansas and Louisville after an Oct. 5 official visit to Lexington and a follow-up in-home from the staff this past week. [Five-star point guard Reese Alston picked LSU](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-news/185894/reese-alston-commits-lsu-tigers-uk-basketball-recruiting) over Louisville, Wichita State, Tennessee and Kentucky, despite Pope's prior NBA tie to Alston's father Rafer and assistant Mikhial McLean's connection to Alston's Houston Second Baptist high school. Ryan Hampton remains the only commitment in the 2027 class, with the 5-in-5 rule giving Kentucky flexibility on the wing through potential returns from Momcilovic, Kam Williams, Braydon Hawthorne and Ousmane N'Diaye.

### Game Analysis

Kentucky led 15-11 inside the first media timeout at the United Center before Kansas ripped off a [15-2 run](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401909786) that reordered the night. By the time [Tyran Stokes buried a 24-foot three](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401909786) with just over six minutes left in the half, the Jayhawks were up ten and Kentucky was chasing the game from behind. The halftime margin of 46-36 understated how one-sided the middle stretch of the first half had been.

The second half offered no reset. Kansas opened with a [14-2 run](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401909786) out of the break that stretched a ten-point edge to 65-43, and Kentucky never cut the deficit back inside single digits. A later 8-2 push trimmed it to sixteen, but Tre White and Stokes kept answering from the perimeter. Kansas finished 10-of-26 from three, and the shot chart tells the story cleanly: the Jayhawks took nearly half their attempts at the rim and converted a comfortable share of them.

The four factors sit where the eye test left them. Kentucky [turned it over on 19.2% of its possessions](https://www.barttorvik.com/team.php?team=Kentucky&year=2027) against a Kansas defense that gave up very little on the glass, and the extra possessions on the other end funded the runs. Kentucky's eFG% was livable; the turnover gap was not.

[Zoom Diallo](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401909786) was the one Kentucky player who sustained anything, pouring in 24 on efficient shooting and carrying the offense when the game was already slipping. [Malachi Moreno](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401909786) added a double-double with 19 points and real rim presence. Outside of those two, the Cats shot poorly from distance and struggled to get stops; Milan Momcilovic and Alex Wilkins combined to go cold from the floor, and the bench produced little scoring. Kansas starters Tre White (30) and Stokes (23) were the difference whenever the game threatened to tighten.

Kentucky falls to 2-1 with the neutral-site loss.

## Cats Notebook

### Champions Classic Postmortem

Kansas beat Kentucky 89-69 at the United Center, and the defining number was a [22-0 edge in points off turnovers](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/points-off-turnovers-were-the-difference-for-kansas-in-20-point-win-over-kentucky/) despite the Jayhawks coughing it up 10 times themselves. Kansas also dished [24 assists on 32 made field goals](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/final-tyran-stokes-shines-as-kansas-blows-out-kentucky-89-69-in-champions-classic/) without starting point guard Melvin Council Jr., while Kentucky shot 4-for-21 from three. [Mark Pope](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/hear-mark-popes-explanation-of-kentuckys-champions-classic-loss-to-kansas/) called it an "incredibly frustrating night" and sent no players to the podium, a decision that drew pushback from national writers in Chicago.

### Milan Momcilovic's Usage Problem

Momcilovic attempted just seven shots and finished with five points on 2-for-7 shooting, failing to put up a three-point attempt in the first half. [Pope acknowledged](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-needs-more-shot-attempts-milan-momcilovic/) the staff has to be more creative generating looks for the 6-foot-9 preseason All-American candidate, while also putting some onus on Momcilovic himself. At halftime, Kentucky had [zero three-point attempts from its best shooter](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kansas-leads-kentucky-by-double-digits-at-halftime-in-chicago/), a stat that framed the entire offensive breakdown.

### Zoom Diallo Was the Lone Bright Spot

[Diallo scored 24 points on 10-for-15 shooting](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-news/187475/mvp-box-score-twitter-reactions-kansas-uk-basketball) but did not record an assist, the only Wildcat who played at a high level before the game got out of hand. Malachi Moreno finished with 19 points and 10 rebounds after overcoming early foul trouble, though [most of his production came late](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/final-tyran-stokes-shines-as-kansas-blows-out-kentucky-89-69-in-champions-classic/) with the outcome decided. Freshman Mason Williams was named [SEC Freshman of the Week](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/187406/mason-williams-sec-freshman-of-the-week-award) earlier Monday on the strength of his two season-opening performances.

### Tyran Stokes Made It Personal

The Louisville native and projected 2027 No. 1 pick scored 23 points with 10 rebounds and four assists, shooting 4-for-8 from three after missing his first four attempts. [Stokes told reporters](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/playing-kentucky-personal-freshman-star-tyran-stokes/) the game was personal given his long Kentucky recruitment and his finalist decision between the Jayhawks and Wildcats last spring. [Tyler Thompson reported](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-kansas-ugly-loss-mark-pope-fans-questions/) that Stokes wanted a late dunk specifically so he could "celebrate a little more," and he directed trash talk at the Kentucky bench and at Pope throughout the second half.

### The Pattern Under Pope

The loss dropped Kentucky to [2-8 in its last 10 Champions Classic appearances](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/187503/mark-pope-struggle-good-teams) and extended a troubling trend, as Kentucky has now trailed by 17 or more in four straight games against Top 25 competition. Fans [hit the exits at the under-eight timeout](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-kansas-ugly-loss-mark-pope-fans-questions/), unusual behavior at a neutral-site event, and A Sea of Blue's recap questioned [whether the Pope era is already on the brink](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/187490/kansas-jayhawks-final-score-recap-3-things-to-know-mourning). Kentucky returns home to face Northern Arizona on Friday.

### Recruiting Note

Kentucky remains in a two-team battle with North Carolina for [5-star 2027 power forward CJ Rosser](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/187448/cj-rosser-kentucky-north-carolina-recruit), the No. 2 overall player in his class, who took an official visit to Lexington in October after visiting Chapel Hill in September. The pursuit follows last week's commitment from 4-star 7-foot-4 center Cherif Millogo.

### Game Analysis

[Alex Wilkins's 24-foot three with two seconds left](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401921803), off a Mason Williams feed, flipped a one-possession deficit into a 78-77 win at Rupp Arena. Twenty seconds earlier, Cade Bennett's 25-foot three had put Northern Arizona up four and looked like the dagger. Chris Komin's layup inside a minute to play and Ryan Abelman's 30-footer in the final four minutes had each pushed the Lumberjacks ahead, and Kentucky needed the final possession to escape.

The game turned early in the second half, when Northern Arizona ripped off a 14-2 run out of the break to erase a 45-37 halftime cushion and briefly take the lead. Kentucky answered with a 9-2 run capped by [Justin McBride's 24-foot three off a Malachi Moreno assist](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401921803), nudging the hosts back in front before the frantic closing exchanges. The second half ended 40-33 the other way after a 45-point first half, a reminder that Kentucky's offense did most of its work before intermission.

The shot chart tells the story of why this stayed close. Northern Arizona went 13-for-26 from three, matching Kentucky's volume from deep and taking half its attempts from beyond the arc. Komin carried the visitors with a 25-point, 6-assist line. Kentucky's defense gave up too much from distance to pull away despite a tidy 6.5% turnover rate that kept possessions flowing one way.

Four Kentucky starters finished with 11 or more, and the offense hummed at a 66.4 eFG%. [Moreno's six assists](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401921803) stood out from the center spot, pairing with three blocks and efficient finishing inside. McBride was perfect off the bench on limited touches, and Ousmane N'Diaye hit three triples in a reserve-heavy role. Zoom Diallo was quieter than his early-season norm but chipped in a pair of steals.

Kentucky moves to 3-1 with the W1 streak intact after surviving a Q4 scare in front of 19,459.

## Cats Notebook

### Survival Over Northern Arizona

Kentucky escaped Rupp Arena with a 78-77 win over an 0-3 Northern Arizona team picked last in the Big Sky, saved by [Alex Wilkins' step-back three with 2.1 seconds left](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/final-alex-wilkins-saves-kentucky-from-stunning-upset-wildcats-steal-78-77-win-over-nau/) after the Lumberjacks front-ended back-to-back one-and-ones. Wilkins finished with 14 points, tied with Milan Momcilovic and Malachi Moreno for the team lead, with five Wildcats reaching double figures. The Cats shot 56.4 percent from the field, went 11-of-26 from three, and posted a [20-to-4 assist-to-turnover ratio](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentuckys-defensive-execution-incredibly-poor-against-northern-arizona/), yet needed late heroics against a team ranked No. 299 at KenPom entering the night.

### Rebounding and Defensive Collapse

Northern Arizona outrebounded Kentucky 32-20, including an 8-1 edge on the offensive glass that produced 11 second-chance points, with [Kentucky's lone offensive rebound not coming until the second half](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-had-one-offensive-rebound-vs-northern-arizona/). Mark Pope called the defensive execution "incredibly poor" and acknowledged [slippage in the team's defensive identity](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentuckys-defensive-execution-incredibly-poor-against-northern-arizona/), with Chris Komin torching the Cats for 25 points on 5-of-8 three-point shooting and Lucas Toukam adding 18.

### Pope's Postgame Tone

Asked for a message to the fan base after the near-upset, Pope said [he hoped everyone at Rupp had fun](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-hopes-everybody-had-fun-watching-an-exciting-game/) and called the game "pretty entertaining, if not well executed." The framing drew sharp pushback from [A Sea of Blue's Drew Holbrook](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-news/187907/mark-pope-trolling-uk-basketball-fans-postgame-remarks), who called the quotes "borderline insulting" given the frustration audible in the arena. Jason Marcum [argued this should be Pope's final season](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/187800/mark-pope-head-coach-this-should-be-last-season) regardless of how the year finishes.

### Rotation and Cam Williams

Cam Williams did not play a single second against Northern Arizona, with Pope saying [Williams is healthy but being worked back "gently"](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kam-williams-healthy-but-didnt-play-second-vs-northern-arizona/) after multiple foot surgeries. Williams logged nine minutes against James Madison and four against Kansas before the DNP.

### KenPom Freefall

Kentucky entered Friday at No. 36 and [fell to No. 51 after the win](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-drops-to-no-51-in-kenpom-now-projected-to-finish-17-15/), with the offense ranked 17th and the defense 114th. KenPom now projects a 17-15 finish, which would be the program's lowest Pomeroy ranking since the 9-16 COVID season of 2020-21. Northern Arizona, ranked 299th before the game, moved up to 264th after nearly stealing one at Rupp.

### What's Next

Grambling State visits Rupp on Monday, followed by a trip to Indianapolis to face Indiana at Lucas Oil Stadium on Friday.

### Game Analysis

Manhattan hung around for the first four minutes, leading 13-9 before [Alex Wilkins answered with a 25-foot three](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401921801) to flip the margin. From there Kentucky ripped off an 18-2 run, with [Justin McBride's 23-foot three](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401921801) putting the hosts ahead to stay. A second 12-2 run inside the final five minutes of the half pushed the lead to 26, and an 11-2 run bridging halftime stretched it past 30.

The shot diet told the story. Kentucky took more than half its field-goal attempts from behind the arc and still finished the night shooting 53 percent from the floor, buoyed by converting better than four of every five looks at the rim. Manhattan, by contrast, could not generate anything efficient against the Kentucky defense, settling for a heavy diet of threes that would not fall.

[Milan Momcilovic](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401921801) led all scorers with 24 points, carrying the perimeter work in a half-court offense that moved the ball cleanly for 20 assists. [Wilkins](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401921801) filled the box score in every column, pairing his 11 points with seven steals and five assists. [Malachi Moreno](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401921801) worked the glass for a 10-rebound night, and Zoom Diallo, Braydon Hawthorne, Franck Kepnang and Mason Williams all chipped in off a balanced rotation.

Novak Manojlovic and Kevin Kearney each reached 11 for the Jaspers. Manhattan's late 13-2 run against the deep bench inside the final five minutes merely trimmed the cosmetic margin after the game had long since been decided. Kentucky entered with a [116.7 adjusted offensive rating per Bart Torvik](https://www.barttorvik.com/team.php?team=Kentucky&year=2027), and the opener did little to contradict the profile.

Kentucky moves to 1-0 with the 85-57 win in front of 19,304 at Rupp Arena.

## Cats Notebook

### Season Opener Takeaways

Kentucky opened with an [85-57 win over Manhattan](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/final-milan-momcilovic-shines-as-no-23-kentucky-takes-season-opener-vs-manhattan/) that turned decisive after a sluggish start of seven points in the first seven minutes. The Cats shot 53.3 percent from the field and 38.7 percent from three, holding Manhattan to 35.2 percent and forcing 13 turnovers. Milan Momcilovic poured in 24 points on 6-of-9 from deep, [tying Antonio Reeves' program record for threes made in a Kentucky debut](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/milan-momcilovic-and-alex-wilkins-make-kentucky-history-in-their-debuts/). Alex Wilkins added 11 points and [set a new program record for steals in a debut with seven](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/milan-momcilovic-and-alex-wilkins-make-kentucky-history-in-their-debuts/), breaking Rajon Rondo's previous mark of five.

### Coach Pope Quotes

Pope was pleased with the 20 assists and 14 offensive rebounds but [pointed to second-half slippage as unacceptable](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/watch-mark-pope-kentucky-players-recap-season-opening-win/), noting a stretch with three turnovers in four possessions. On Momcilovic's gravity, Pope said [he is "like the sun" and pulls defenders away from teammates](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186519/milan-momcilovic-uk-history-debut-manhattan). Asked whether his demeanor has shifted this season, Pope told reporters [he is "just getting better at this"](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/watch-mark-pope-kentucky-players-recap-season-opening-win/) rather than conceding a change in approach.

### Rotation and Role Players

Zoom Diallo answered a rough preseason with 12 points, three rebounds and two assists in 27 minutes, though he also committed four turnovers. The Rupp crowd rewarded him with a "Zoom" chant in the second half, and Diallo [thanked fans afterward for bringing out "that joy that I needed"](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/zoom-diallo-thanks-fans-for-support-kentucky-manhattan/). Franck Kepnang, in his first real action after averaging just 7.3 minutes across three scoreless exhibitions, [gave Pope what the coach called some of his best minutes yet](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/franck-kepnang-showed-valuable-be-bench/), punctuated by a lane dunk. Braydon Hawthorne finished with eight points, five rebounds, four assists and two blocks in 19 minutes, drawing a [Kevin Durant comparison from Momcilovic](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/braydon-hawthorne-flashed-more-potential-vs-manhattan-reminds-me-of-kevin-durant-a-little-bit/) tied to his length and mid-post feel. Malachi Moreno nearly posted a double-double with eight points and 10 rebounds, with five offensive boards in the first half.

### Recruiting Signal

With the early signing period starting next week, Pope told reporters Kentucky has been [active with high school freshmen but is being "incredibly intentional"](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/watch-mark-pope-kentucky-players-recap-season-opening-win/) about fit, timing and price. National analyst Joe Tipton sent a tweet during the Manhattan game that A Sea of Blue interpreted as [bullish on Kentucky's standing with Cherif Millogo](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186522/uk-recruiting-expert-good-new-coming-soon-cherif-millogo), who is ranked as high as No. 8 by ESPN and No. 11 by Rivals and would project as a potential replacement for Moreno if the sophomore turns pro.

### Up Next

The Cats host James Madison on Friday, which brings former Wildcat Justin McBride against his old program under head coach Preston Spradlin. Pope downplayed the reunion angle as routine in modern college basketball but [called Spradlin "one of the great human beings in the coaching profession"](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/watch-mark-pope-kentucky-players-recap-season-opening-win/) and a Kentucky guy he has had long basketball and spiritually based conversations with over the years.

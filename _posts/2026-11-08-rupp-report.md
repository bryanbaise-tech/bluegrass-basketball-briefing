## Cats Notebook

### Champions Classic Framing

The beat has coalesced around Tuesday's Kansas matchup in Chicago as the season's first real referendum on Mark Pope's rebuilt roster. [Zoom Diallo called it a "statement game"](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/zoom-diallo-kansas-is-must-win-game-for-kentucky/) for a team trying to buy early patience after a noisy offseason and hot-seat chatter. The markets are not with Kentucky: [Kansas opened as a 5.5-point favorite on Polymarket, with KenPom giving UK a 27 percent chance and Bart Torvik projecting an 81-74 Jayhawks win](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kansas-opens-as-favorite-over-kentucky-in-champions-classic/). ESPN's Matchup Predictor is slightly friendlier at 35.5 percent. [A Sea of Blue frames the Tyran Stokes subplot as the central drama](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/187117/kansas-jayhawks-time-tv-channel-online-stream-predictions-odds), noting Kentucky's staff concentrated its 2026 recruiting chips on the Louisville native before he committed to Bill Self.

### Defensive Concerns After James Madison

Friday's 89-80 win over James Madison left a visible flaw on tape heading into Kansas. [The Dukes launched 45 three-pointers, tying a program record for a Kentucky opponent](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/187110/james-madison-launching-a-record-tying-45-3s-vs-kentucky-exposes-a-glaring-defensive-reality), connecting on 15 and keeping the game tied at halftime before Kentucky separated late. The only other opponent to reach that volume was Tennessee in 2025. The implication for Tuesday is obvious given Kansas's perimeter talent, and the beat is treating the three-point defense as the first thing Pope needs to tighten.

### Former Cats Elsewhere

Two spring departures are producing early. [Jaland Lowe opened his Georgetown career with 20-plus points in each of his first two games, including 22 points, eight assists and five rebounds against Maryland on 5-of-8 three-point shooting](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/jaland-lowe-is-off-to-a-hot-start-for-georgetown/). In the NBA, [Shai Gilgeous-Alexander averaged 35.8 points on the week and hit the go-ahead three against Cleveland](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/bbnba-shai-gilgeous-alexander-is-already-in-midseason-form/), carrying extra load for Oklahoma City with Cason Wallace sidelined by a torn labrum.

### Home Slate Promotions

Kentucky confirmed the [1996 championship-inspired denim jerseys will return for the December 12 Louisville game](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-mbb-bringing-back-denim-jerseys-plus-other-in-game-themes-promotions/), which has also been branded a White Out. The release listed halftime acts across the home schedule including Red Panda on January 23 and the Kings Firecrackers Jump Rope Team on February 6.

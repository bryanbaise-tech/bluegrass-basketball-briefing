## Cats Notebook

### Exhibition Takeaways and UCF Up Next

No. 23 Kentucky returns to Rupp on Wednesday for its final exhibition against UCF after an [impressive 76-65 win over No. 19 Texas Tech](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185624/ucf-knights-game-time-tv-channel-live-online-stream-what-to-watch) in which five players reached double figures. The Texas Tech showing offered early signs that younger pieces are pushing for rotation minutes in Mark Pope's third season, though the staff is tempering optimism given last year's exhibition demolition of Purdue preceded a disappointing finish. Pope himself used Monday's pre-Texas Tech availability to [address the team](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xC8G4k1Fx_8) before the Red Raiders game.

### 2027 Recruiting Board Heats Up

The Wildcats are positioned at the center of two of the class of 2027's biggest decisions this week. [DeMarcus Henry](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185734/demarcus-henry-college-decision-date-uk-recruiting-north-carolina), the No. 2 player in America per Rivals, will choose Friday from a final four of Kentucky, Arkansas, Louisville, and North Carolina after visiting Lexington earlier this month for Pro Day. On3's Joe Tipton has [logged a prediction for Kentucky to land 7-foot-5 Cherif Millogo](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185663/cherif-millogo-recruiting-expert-predictions-uk-byu), now ranked as high as No. 8 overall by ESPN, with BYU still in the mix. Millogo took a surprise visit to campus on October 9, shortly before Kentucky hired former NBA Academy Africa coach Alfred Aboya, who previously worked with him.

### Looking Ahead to 2028

The pipeline work extends into the next cycle. Top-10 2028 target [Brady Pettigrew rescheduled his visit](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185674/brady-pettigrew-uk-recruiting-visits-louisville-cardinals-game) to the weekend of December 12 and will be in Rupp for the Louisville game, per 247 Sports. Pettigrew won the Nike EYBL's first 16u MVP after averaging more than 24 points per game and hit the game-winner in the 16u Peach Jam final. Illinois is viewed as the current favorite for the in-state combo guard, who is also considered a reclass candidate for 2027.

### Former Cats in the NBA

The news from Oklahoma City turned grim for two recent Wildcats. [Cason Wallace](https://www.aseaofblue.com/former-kentucky-wildcats/185700/cason-wallace-injury-surgery-recovery-timeline-oklahoma-city-thunder) suffered a torn labrum in his left shoulder and will undergo surgery, with a return this season looking unlikely after he signed a lucrative extension before the year. The Thunder also [waived Otega Oweh](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-news/185658/kentucky-wildcats-news-otega-oweh-waived-and-uk-vs-ucf-basketball-week), the 41st overall pick last summer, after he ruptured his Achilles just before the regular season.

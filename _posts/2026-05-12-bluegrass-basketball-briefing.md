## Cats Notebook

### Roster Construction

Kentucky's frontcourt picture firmed up when [Franck Kepnang received a medical hardship waiver](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172156/franck-kepnang-eligible-for-kentucky-for-seventh-and-final-season) clearing him for a seventh and final college season. The 6-foot-11, 253-pound Washington transfer averaged 6.2 points, 6.3 rebounds, and 2.1 blocks last year on 51.5% shooting, profiling as a defense-and-rebounding backup behind Malachi Moreno. Brandon Ramsey's [film study on Justin McBride](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/film-room-justin-mcbride/) framed the James Madison transfer as a high-floor stretch four whose 40% three-point shooting and pick-and-pop feel fit Pope's system, even as the commitment announcement was overshadowed by Tyran Stokes picking Kansas. Kyle Wiltjer, who played against Ousmane N'Diaye twice in Italy this season, [told Tom Leach](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kyle-wiltjer-says-fans-should-be-excited-about-ousmane-ndiaye-and-the-future-of-kentucky-basketball/) the 6-foot-11 Senegalese forward "can shoot really well" and finishes strong at the rim against grown professionals.

### The Moreno Decision

Malachi Moreno's draft process is now the central variable for 2026-27. [Moreno will not participate in the combine's five-on-five scrimmages](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/malachi-moreno-will-not-scrimmage-at-the-nba-draft-combine-after-all/), a development Kevin O'Connor framed as Moreno having "garnered intrigue from teams in the mid-late first round range." O'Connor ranks him 30th on his big board and called him "one of the clear risers in the pre-draft process." [Moreno's official measurements](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/see-how-malachi-moreno-measured-in-at-the-nba-draft-combine/) came in at 6-foot-11.25 barefoot, 242.8 pounds, with a 7-foot-1.5 wingspan and 9-foot-3.5 standing reach. Sea of Blue's Jason Marcum noted there is now [a "pretty strong chance" Moreno gets drafted in the first round](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172161/malachi-moreno-nba-draft-combine-scrimmages-first-round-pick), with the May 27 withdrawal deadline as the hard checkpoint. Pope has been public about wanting him back, [telling fans in his Q&A](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-says-he-ended-a-portal-visit-after-three-hours-in-final-offseason-bbn-qa/) that Moreno was the offseason's top priority and projecting him as the best center in college basketball next year.

### Pursuing a Star Wing

With Tyran Stokes gone to Kansas, the beat is converging on Iowa State's Milan Momcilovic as the program's last realistic swing at a headline addition. [Jack Pilgrim wrote that Pope is hoping to steal](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/all-eyes-are-on-milan-momcilovic-at-the-nba-draft-combine-in-chicago/) the 6-foot-8 sharpshooter, who hit 48.7% from three while averaging 16.9 points as a junior and dropped 20 on the Cats in the round of 32. Momcilovic measured 6-foot-8 barefoot with a 6-foot-9.25 wingspan and shot [17-of-25 in spot-up drills and 22-of-30 off the dribble at the combine](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/how-the-kentucky-wildcats-shot-the-ball-at-the-nba-combine-results-moreno-oweh-momcilovic-quaintance/).

### Coach Pope Q&A

Pope answered another round of fan questions on social media in what amounts to his most direct engagement with Big Blue Nation this offseason. He [confirmed Kentucky went all-in on a "generational talent"](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-says-he-ended-a-portal-visit-after-three-hours-in-final-offseason-bbn-qa/) and fell short, acknowledged Kentucky gets used as NIL leverage because of brand size, and described ending one portal visit after three hours when the fit wasn't right. On the center spot, Pope said Moreno worked out for the Knicks and called him a player chasing two dreams simultaneously. Asked specifically about Cam Williams by Locked On Kentucky's Lance Dawe, [Pope said yes, Williams is ready to start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DtqPvlNVQ1s), citing him as the team's leading defensive points allowed and plus-minus player despite missing 12 games, and noting Williams texted him a scale photo at 210 pounds.

### Front Office

Keegan Brown [opened up on social media about his role](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172032/keegan-brown-role-job) in Pope's reshaped basketball operations staff after speculation that he might be the program's general manager. Brown, hired in late March from the Wisconsin Herd after a previous stint with Pope at BYU, described his work as analytics-anchored but expanding into agent conversations, model-building with Nick Robinson, contract management, and film, rather than a single GM title. Wildcat Blue Nation's Lane Mills characterized Pope's broader [answer on the GM question as confusing](https://wildcatbluenation.com/mark-pope-gave-a-confusing-answer-to-kentucky-s-hot-button-general-manager-question-01kr49n51fw2), and the structure remains a point of friction with fans who want a clearer single decision-maker on roster construction.

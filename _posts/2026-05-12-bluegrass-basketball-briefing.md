## Cats Notebook

### Roster Construction and the Center Position

[Franck Kepnang](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172156/franck-kepnang-eligible-for-kentucky-for-seventh-and-final-season) was granted eligibility for a seventh and final season, locking in a 6-foot-11, 253-pound backup five who averaged 6.2 points, 6.3 rebounds, and 2.1 blocks at Washington last year. [Kyle Wiltjer told Tom Leach](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kyle-wiltjer-says-fans-should-be-excited-about-ousmane-ndiaye-and-the-future-of-kentucky-basketball/) that incoming forward Ousmane N'Diaye, whom he played against twice in Italy's Serie A, can shoot it well, is super athletic defensively, and finishes strong at the rim. James Madison transfer Justin McBride [shot 40% from three on 95 attempts](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/film-room-justin-mcbride/) and projects as a stretch four whose commitment was overshadowed by the Tyran Stokes news arriving an hour later.

### The Malachi Moreno Decision

The combine has converted what looked like a layup return into a real coin flip. [Moreno will not scrimmage in Chicago](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/malachi-moreno-will-not-scrimmage-at-the-nba-draft-combine-after-all/), with Kevin O'Connor noting he has garnered intrigue from teams in the mid-to-late first round and slotting him at No. 39 in his post-lottery mock while ranking him 30th on his big board. He [measured 6-11.25 barefoot at 242.8 pounds](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/see-how-malachi-moreno-measured-in-at-the-nba-draft-combine/) with a 7-1.5 wingspan and 9-3.5 standing reach, slightly shorter than his Pro Day numbers from the fall. [Pope said in his Q&A](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-says-he-ended-a-portal-visit-after-three-hours-in-final-offseason-bbn-qa/) that Moreno was the program's number one priority entering portal season and that the staff believes he will be the best center in college basketball next year, while supporting both his banner and NBA dreams. The May 27 withdrawal deadline is the date to circle.

### The Momcilovic Pursuit

Iowa State sharpshooter Milan Momcilovic, who [dropped 20 on Kentucky in the Round of 32](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/all-eyes-are-on-milan-momcilovic-at-the-nba-draft-combine-in-chicago/), sits atop Pope's wish list as the star addition to fill the wing vacancy originally reserved for Stokes. At the combine he [hit 17 of 25 in spot-up shooting and 16 of 25 in the 3-point star drill](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/how-the-kentucky-wildcats-shot-the-ball-at-the-nba-combine-results-moreno-oweh-momcilovic-quaintance/), and he measured 6-8 barefoot at 218.2 pounds. The pitch, if he withdraws from the draft, will be a starring role and a substantial NIL package; the question is whether his pro buzz outpaces what Kentucky can put on the table.

### Coach Pope Goes Public

After weeks of criticism for staying quiet, [Pope ended a portal visit after three hours](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-says-he-ended-a-portal-visit-after-three-hours-in-final-offseason-bbn-qa/) in a story he shared during his fourth installment of fan Q&A videos, addressing recruiting misses, agent relationships, and the GM question. On the Stokes pursuit specifically, Pope acknowledged the program was chasing a generational talent and fell short.

### Quaintance and Oweh at the Combine

Former Wildcat Jayden Quaintance [measured 6-9 barefoot at 253 pounds with a 7-5.25 wingspan](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/jayden-quaintance-posts-impressive-measurements-at-nba-draft-combine/), and Jonathan Givony called his dimensions extremely impressive for an NBA big man. [Otega Oweh went 23 of 30 in shooting off the dribble](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/how-the-kentucky-wildcats-shot-the-ball-at-the-nba-combine-results-moreno-oweh-momcilovic-quaintance/), eighth among combine participants, and posted a 12-of-25 spot-up mark.

### Backcourt Endorsement

New assistant Mo Williams, who recruited both incoming guards, [predicted Zoom Diallo will land on one of the three All-SEC teams](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mo-williams-predicts-big-things-from-zoom-diallo-alex-wilkins-kentucky/) and raved about Alex Wilkins alongside him. Diallo averaged 15.7 points and 4.5 assists as a sophomore at Washington before transferring in April. Williams framed the duo as a fit-built backcourt that will create headaches in conference play.

## Cats Notebook

### Roster Construction

The Cats added Washington transfer center [Franck Kepnang](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172156/franck-kepnang-eligible-for-kentucky-for-seventh-and-final-season), who received eligibility for his seventh and final season and brings 6-foot-11, 253-pound frontcourt depth behind Malachi Moreno, averaging 6.2 points, 6.3 rebounds, and 2.1 blocks per game at Washington last season. The signing solidifies the backup five spot after Kentucky [missed on Marcio Santos](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/sources-say-goes-live-to-talk-malachi-moreno-draft-buzz-and-kentuckys-roster-rebuild/), who chose LSU. [Kyle Wiltjer told Tom Leach](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kyle-wiltjer-says-fans-should-be-excited-about-ousmane-ndiaye-and-the-future-of-kentucky-basketball/) that Senegalese forward Ousmane N'Diaye, who Wiltjer played against twice in Italy's Serie A this season, can shoot well, is super athletic defensively, and finishes strong at the rim. Roster analyst Keegan Brown [described his role on Twitter](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172032/keegan-brown-role-job) as building models with Nick Robinson, managing contracts, grinding film, and running the front office systems, which the staff is using in place of a traditional GM hire.

### The Malachi Moreno Decision

[Moreno will not participate](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/malachi-moreno-will-not-scrimmage-at-the-nba-draft-combine-after-all/) in the NBA Draft Combine five-on-five scrimmages, with The Ringer's Kevin O'Connor reporting a pretty strong chance Moreno gets drafted in the first round. O'Connor has Moreno going 39th to Houston in his post-lottery mock but ranks him 30th on his big board and called him one of the clear risers in the pre-draft process. Moreno [measured 6-foot-11.25 barefoot, 242.8 pounds with a 7-foot-1.5 wingspan and 9-foot-3.5 standing reach](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/see-how-malachi-moreno-measured-in-at-the-nba-draft-combine/) at the combine, per Jon Chepkevich of Draft Express. Moreno has a return deal signed with Kentucky and the May 27 withdrawal deadline now becomes the pivot point for the entire 2026-27 roster.

### Wing Pursuit and Mark Pope's Q&A

With the post-Tyran Stokes star vacancy still open on the wing, [Pope is targeting Iowa State's Milan Momcilovic](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/all-eyes-are-on-milan-momcilovic-at-the-nba-draft-combine-in-chicago/), the 6-foot-8 sharpshooter who hit 48.7 percent from three while averaging 16.9 points and dropped 20 on the Cats in the Round of 32. Pope [acknowledged in his fourth offseason Q&A video](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-says-he-ended-a-portal-visit-after-three-hours-in-final-offseason-bbn-qa/) that Kentucky chased a generational talent and fell short but said the staff was not sitting on its hands, and he confirmed Moreno had a workout with the Knicks last week. On assistant Mo Williams' new backcourt, [Williams predicted Zoom Diallo will land on one of the three All-SEC teams](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mo-williams-predicts-big-things-from-zoom-diallo-alex-wilkins-kentucky/) and called the Diallo–Alex Wilkins pairing an ideal fit.

### Combine Showings for Former Cats

Otega Oweh [went 23-of-30 in shooting off the dribble](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/how-the-kentucky-wildcats-shot-the-ball-at-the-nba-combine-results-moreno-oweh-momcilovic-quaintance/), good for eighth overall, while Moreno hit 12-of-25 in spot-up and 17-of-30 off the dribble. [Jayden Quaintance measured 6-foot-9 barefoot and 253 pounds](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/jayden-quaintance-posts-impressive-measurements-at-nba-draft-combine/) with a 7-foot-5.25 wingspan and 9-foot-1 standing reach, with Jonathan Givony of Draft Express noting Quaintance grew an inch over the past two years and has extremely impressive dimensions for an NBA big man. Quaintance was limited to four games at Kentucky due to a knee injury.

### Game Analysis

Kentucky broke a tied game open with a [13-2 run](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401921802) across the first four minutes of the second half, turning a 54-56 deficit into a 67-58 lead that James Madison never recovered from. Mason Williams started it with a 16-foot turnaround, [Alex Wilkins](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401921802) answered a Paul Jones III three with a 23-footer of his own, and Justin McBride's 26-foot three off a Williams feed pushed the margin to six and the game largely out of reach.

The first half had been a track meet, with both sides putting up 39 and the Dukes closing on a late push to take the game into the break tied. James Madison lived behind the arc all night, hoisting two-thirds of its field goal attempts from three.

The efficiency gap is where the game was decided. Kentucky finished at [58.7% effective field goal percentage](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401921802) against a James Madison defense that could not get stops when it mattered, and the hosts matched the Dukes' low turnover rate so the volume battle never materialized. An 8-1 stretch closed the door after James Madison had trimmed it earlier.

Diallo and Wilkins paced the offense from the backcourt, while McBride was the most efficient Wildcat on the floor, pouring in a career afternoon off the bench with eight boards to boot. Malachi Moreno anchored the interior with a pair of blocks, and Braydon Hawthorne chipped in at the rim. Brandon Lee did most of his damage at the line for the Dukes and had help from Garrett Sundra's perimeter shooting.

In front of 19,292 at Rupp Arena, Kentucky moved to 2-0.

## Cats Notebook

### Backcourt Protects the Ball

Kentucky's three ball handlers combined for zero turnovers across 77 minutes against James Madison, [dishing eight assists](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-backcourt-trio-didnt-log-single-turnover-vs-james-madison/) after Zoom Diallo, Alex Wilkins, and Mason Williams had committed nine giveaways two games ago. Pope singled out JMU's pressure as the reason the clean sheet mattered, noting the Dukes [get up into the line](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-backcourt-trio-didnt-log-single-turnover-vs-james-madison/) rather than sitting back. Diallo's 16 points on 7-of-11 shooting in 22 minutes came after what A Sea of Blue described as a [digital detox](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/187005/zoom-diallo-best-game-wildcat-james-madison-box-score-uk) following online criticism of his preseason play and four-turnover opener against Manhattan.

### Defense Takes a Step Back

The Dukes [launched 45 three-pointers and made 15](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/ksrs-takeaways-from-kentuckys-too-close-for-comfort-win-over-jmu/), getting clean looks repeatedly against a Kentucky defense that had been the team's early strength through the exhibition slate. Brandon Lee went for [26 points](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/final-no-23-kentucky-survives-early-scare-from-james-madison-with-89-80-win/) to lead JMU, and the game was tied at halftime with the Dukes having already taken 26 threes by the break.

### Bench Carries the Night

Justin McBride, Mason Williams, and Braydon Hawthorne [combined for 35 of Kentucky's 43 bench points](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentuckys-bench-came-up-big-in-an-uncomfortable-win-over-james-madison/). McBride's reunion with his former program produced 13 points, eight rebounds, and 3-of-4 from deep in 27 minutes, with Preston Spradlin [joking postgame](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/justin-mcbride-came-up-huge-kentucky-against-former-team/) that he wished McBride had shot it that well for JMU last year. Spradlin [also helped McBride](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/how-preston-spradlin-helped-kentucky-land-justin-mcbride/) land at Kentucky during the portal cycle, telling KSR's Sources Say Podcast he knew the Pikeville native wanted to get closer to home.

### Kam Williams Eases Back In

Kam Williams made his season debut after two foot surgeries, playing nine minutes on what Pope described as a [12-minute limit](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kam-williams-is-back-and-mark-pope-expects-him-to-put-his-fingerprints-on-kentucky/). He went 0-for-3 from the field but hit both free throws during the second-half run that flipped a two-point deficit into a double-digit lead. Jason Marcum noted the significance is less in the box score than in the [timing ahead of Kansas](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186983/kam-williams-returns-injury-james-madison-uk-kansas) in the Champions Classic, where Williams now projects as a rotation piece rather than spot minutes.

### Momcilovic Cools Off

Milan Momcilovic followed his 24-point, 6-of-9 three-point debut with [just seven points](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186971/james-madison-uk-final-score-recap-4-things-to-know) against James Madison, tied for third on the team in shot attempts at halftime before fading. Pilgrim's in-game [live blog note](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-and-james-madison-are-tied-at-halftime-on-the-heels-of-26-3pa-for-the-dukes) questioned the shot distribution with Wilkins sidelined by early foul trouble.

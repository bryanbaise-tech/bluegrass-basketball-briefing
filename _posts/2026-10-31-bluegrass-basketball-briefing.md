## Cats Notebook

### Roster Availability

Mark Pope ruled out Kam Williams for Tuesday's opener against Manhattan, moving the junior wing from week-to-week to [day-to-day](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kam-williams-is-not-expected-to-play-in-season-opener-for-kentucky/) as he works back from a Jones fracture setback. Pope said Williams has been in five-on-zero work in practice and would get a little bit of live work against the scout team in Friday's practice, but Kentucky is operating under extreme caution given the setback from the same injury. Pope's framing on Friday was blunt: "I don't expect to see him touch the floor on Tuesday."

### Zoom Diallo and the Backcourt Conversation

Pope [expressed zero concern](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-is-not-concerned-about-zoom-diallo-or-the-noise-surrounding-his-play/) about Diallo's exhibition production after the Washington transfer finished sixth on the team in scoring at 6.0 points per game on 38/25/20 splits, dishing 4.7 assists against a team-high 2.7 turnovers across three games. Diallo deactivated his Instagram following the UCF finale amid fan criticism, and Pope said after the 90-76 overtime win that Diallo had a 3-to-1 assist-to-turnover ratio in that game while matched against one of the Big 12's top steals guards.

### Mason Williams Earns the Spotlight

The counterpoint to the Diallo discourse has been the freshman guard's rise. Mason Williams averaged 14.0 points on 69.6 percent shooting and 66.7 percent from three across the three exhibitions, hitting a game-tying bucket in the final seconds of regulation against UCF. Pope highlighted the freshman's physicality, confidence, and shot-making, and said Williams "makes the right play nine times out of 10." Pope closed the overtime period with Williams on the floor rather than Diallo, a lineup choice that will carry into the regular season if the freshman keeps producing.

### Malachi Moreno and the Frontcourt

Moreno's offseason conditioning work paid off against UCF, where he logged 37 minutes and finished with 16 points, 14 rebounds, and eight assists while narrowly missing a triple-double. Pope called the minutes total the most impressive part of the stat line, and Moreno credited a spring and summer focused on conditioning and physicality. The sophomore center said longtime mentor [Amari Williams](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EEGqg3LJvN0&t=201s) has helped him learn Pope's offense, which runs heavily through the five.

### Recruiting: Henry Picks UNC

Kentucky missed on 2027 No. 2 overall prospect [DeMarcus Henry](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/5-star-sf-demarcus-henry-commits-to-north-carolina-2/), who committed to Michael Malone and North Carolina over the Wildcats, Louisville, and Arkansas. Henry, originally from Charlotte but who grew up in Santa Ana, CA, took an official visit to Lexington on October 5 and received an in-home visit from Pope's staff the week of his decision. Jack Pilgrim reported Kentucky went 0-for-3 this week on targets, with [Lewis Uvwo picking Providence and Reese Alston committing to LSU](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185895/demarcus-henry-commits-north-carolina-tar-heels-uk-recruiting). The Wildcats' lone 2027 commitment remains wing Ryan Hampton, though Pilgrim reported on [Sources Say](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YoLS9kkF2P0&t=2622s) that 7-foot-5 center Cherif Millogo is expected to commit next week, with Joe Tipton logging a prediction for Kentucky.

## Cats Notebook

### Melvin Council Out Reshapes the Kansas Matchup

Kansas will be without starting guard Melvin Council Jr. in Tuesday's Champions Classic after he [broke the fifth metatarsal in his right foot during Monday's practice](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kansas-standout-melvin-council-jr-breaks-foot-out-vs-kentucky/), according to Bill Self, who said Council will have surgery in the coming days and is hoped to return around the start of Big 12 play. Council was averaging 8.0 points, 5.0 assists, 3.5 rebounds, and 2.5 steals through two games after earning Big 12 Newcomer of the Year honors last season at 12.7 points and 5.1 assists per contest. The absence pushes Toledo transfer Leroy Bladen Jr. into more on-ball responsibility.

### Reading the Kansas Front Court

Even without Council, Kansas enters with serious size and efficiency. Trey Kaufman-Renn is 11-of-13 from the field through two games and gives Bill Self a proven interior scorer. Jeff Goodman reported on the Field of 68 that [Kaufman-Renn is expected to play barring late developments](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ES1fGWdrRLQ&t=3469s), and that Kansas remains the more talented team on paper despite Council's injury.

### Mason Williams Earns SEC Freshman of the Week

Three-star point guard Mason Williams was named the [SEC's first Freshman of the Week of 2026-27](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/187406/mason-williams-sec-freshman-of-the-week-award) after averaging 9.5 points and 5.0 assists in wins over Manhattan and James Madison. The 6-foot-3 Dallas native is shooting 57.1% from the field and 60.0% from three, and his 5.0 assists per game rank second among SEC freshmen. He finished with zero turnovers against James Madison, becoming [the second Pope-era freshman to win the award](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentuckys-mason-williams-wins-sec-freshman-of-the-week/), joining Malachi Moreno.

### Kam Williams Returns, Rotation Questions Remain

Kam Williams made his season debut against James Madison, playing nine minutes on what Mark Pope described as a 12-minute restriction. He scored two points on free throws and missed all three shots, but his return gives Pope another wing for a Kansas game that will require options against Stokes. The James Madison game also [revealed defensive breakdowns](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/187110/james-madison-launching-a-record-tying-45-3s-vs-kentucky-exposes-a-glaring-defensive-reality) that the Dukes exploited for 45 three-point attempts, tying a program record for a Kentucky opponent. Zoom Diallo scored 16 points on 7-of-11 shooting with zero turnovers but did not play the final 14 minutes, a rotation decision Pope did not explain postgame.

### Poll Movement and Scheduling Context

Kentucky dropped one spot to [No. 24 in the AP Top 25](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/where-kentucky-sits-in-the-latest-ap-top-25-following-2-0-start/) and fell out of the Coaches Poll entirely after the 2-0 start. Kansas climbed two spots to No. 15 in the AP poll following wins over Fordham and Middle Tennessee. Tipoff at the United Center is set for 9:30 p.m. ET on ESPN.

## Cats Notebook

### NBA Combine and Moreno's Decision

Malachi Moreno measured 6-foot-11.25 barefoot at 242.8 pounds with a [7-foot-1.5 wingspan and 9-foot-3.5 standing reach](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/see-how-malachi-moreno-measured-in-at-the-nba-draft-combine/) in Chicago. He then [pulled out of the 5-on-5 scrimmages](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/malachi-moreno-will-not-scrimmage-at-the-nba-draft-combine-after-all/), which The Ringer's Kevin O'Connor framed as a sign Moreno has garnered intrigue from teams in the mid-to-late first round. O'Connor now puts a [pretty strong chance](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172161/malachi-moreno-nba-draft-combine-scrimmages-first-round-pick) on Moreno being drafted in the first round, and Wildcat Blue Nation reports Kentucky's [roster construction is effectively frozen](https://wildcatbluenation.com/kentucky-s-roster-is-on-hold-until-malachi-moreno-makes-his-nba-draft-choice-01krbz4frbv6?utm_source=RSS) until his stay-or-go decision. Former Wildcat Jayden Quaintance, who will not return to Lexington, [measured 6-foot-9 barefoot and 253 pounds with a 7-foot-5.25 wingspan](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/jayden-quaintance-posts-impressive-measurements-at-nba-draft-combine/), dimensions Draft Express called extremely impressive for an NBA big.

### Frontcourt Insurance: Kepnang Cleared

Washington transfer Franck Kepnang [received a medical hardship waiver](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/franck-kepnang-receives-waiver-to-play-for-kentucky-basketball-2026-27/) for a seventh and final college season, the university confirmed to KSR. The 6-foot-11 center averaged [6.2 points, 6.3 rebounds, and 2.1 blocks on 51.5% shooting](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172156/franck-kepnang-eligible-for-kentucky-for-seventh-and-final-season) in 22.9 minutes per game at Washington last season, where he played alongside incoming Kentucky guard Zoom Diallo. His college career has been pocked with injury setbacks, including an ACL tear that ended his first Washington season after eight games, which is why A Sea of Blue notes Kepnang projects as a [suitable backup rather than a player Kentucky can lean on for a full slate](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172161/malachi-moreno-nba-draft-combine-scrimmages-first-round-pick) if Moreno departs.

### Backcourt Buy-In from Mo Williams

New assistant Mo Williams played a central role in landing Zoom Diallo and Alex Wilkins, and he [predicted Diallo will land on one of the three All-SEC teams](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mo-williams-predicts-big-things-from-zoom-diallo-alex-wilkins-kentucky/) by season's end. Diallo averaged 15.7 points and 4.5 assists as a sophomore at Washington. Wildcat Blue Nation echoed the framing, casting Williams's read as a [significant endorsement of Kentucky's new floor general](https://wildcatbluenation.com/mo-williams-expects-zoom-diallo-to-be-an-all-sec-contender-at-kentucky-01krckh0rq0y?utm_source=RSS).

### Pope's Q&A and the Portal Process

Mark Pope wrapped his offseason fan Q&A series with a fourth installment that addressed both light topics and program substance. Pope said he [ended one portal recruiting visit after three hours](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-says-he-ended-a-portal-visit-after-three-hours-in-final-offseason-bbn-qa/) when the fit was wrong, and he touched on the 3-point shooting emphasis, agent relationships, and high school recruiting misses. Behind the scenes, new analytics and roster construction hire Keegan Brown described his role on social media as [building models, managing contracts, and working with agents](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172032/keegan-brown-role-job) alongside Nick Robinson, settling speculation that he would carry a GM title. The Milan Momcilovic pursuit, meanwhile, looks like it is [trending against Kentucky](https://wildcatbluenation.com/milan-momcilovic-update-espn-insider-provides-news-kentucky-fans-didn-t-want-to-hear?utm_source=RSS) per ESPN reporting cited by Wildcat Blue Nation, narrowing Pope's remaining swings for a high-end addition.

### Ousmane N'Diaye Scouting Report

Former Wildcat Kyle Wiltjer, who faced Ousmane N'Diaye twice in Italy last season, told Tom Leach that Kentucky fans [should be excited about the 6-foot-11 Senegalese forward](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kyle-wiltjer-says-fans-should-be-excited-about-ousmane-ndiaye-and-the-future-of-kentucky-basketball/). Wiltjer praised N'Diaye's shooting, defensive athleticism, and finishing at the rim, noting he has held up against grown professionals overseas.

### BBNBA Postseason

Karl-Anthony Towns posted [17 points and 10 rebounds in 20 minutes](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/bbnba-playoffs-karl-anthony-towns-eliminates-tyrese-maxeys-76ers/) as the Knicks completed a sweep of Philadelphia, advancing to a second straight Eastern Conference Finals. Towns ranks [first in rebounds and third in assists](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-news/172121/karl-anthony-towns-nba-eastern-conference-finals-knicks) among players still alive this postseason. Out West, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander [dropped 35 in a 115-110 closeout win](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/bbnba-playoffs-shai-gilgeous-alexander-drops-35-as-thunder-sweep-the-lakers/) over the Lakers, his 26th 30-point playoff game as a Thunder, tying Russell Westbrook for second in franchise history. Tyrese Maxey and Justin Edwards combined to go [1-for-13 from three](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/bbnba-playoffs-karl-anthony-towns-eliminates-tyrese-maxeys-76ers/) in Philadelphia's elimination game.

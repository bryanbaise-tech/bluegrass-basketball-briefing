## Cats Notebook

### Cherif Millogo and the 2027 Class

Mark Pope landed [7-foot-5 center Cherif Millogo](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/7-foot-5-center-cherif-millogo-commits-to-kentucky-2/) on Wednesday morning, the second pledge in Kentucky's 2027 class alongside Ryan Hampton. Millogo is No. 11 overall in the Rivals rankings with a 7-foot-9 wingspan, and his guardian is former UCLA star and NBA veteran Luc Mbah a Moute, who is close with recently hired assistant Alfred Aboya. His high school coach Todd Wolfson [told KSR](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/why-kentucky-cherif-millogos-coach-it-feels-like-home/) the St. Francis staff has studied film of Victor Wembanyama and Giannis Antetokounmpo to build a developmental plan for a player still relatively new to the game.

### AJ Williams Visit

The momentum carries into the weekend. Five-star small forward [AJ Williams will take an official visit to Lexington](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/5-star-sf-aj-williams-will-take-an-official-visit-to-kentucky-this-weekend/), as first reported by Dushawn London of 247Sports. Williams is No. 5 overall in the 2027 class and has already visited Auburn, Mississippi State, Georgia, and BYU, with a scheduled Arkansas visit to see John Calipari falling through last weekend. [A Sea of Blue notes](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186657/aj-williams-visit-uk-recruiting) that BYU, Auburn, and Georgia have generated the most buzz, so Kentucky enters as a late mover with ground to make up.

### Season Opener Takeaways

Kentucky's 85-57 win over Manhattan produced several individual storylines worth tracking. Milan Momcilovic went [6-of-9 from three for 24 points](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186556/kentucky-basketball-3-point-shooting-win-manhattan), tying Antonio Reeves for the most threes made by a Wildcat in a regular-season debut. [Alex Wilkins broke Rajon Rondo's Kentucky debut record](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186653/alex-wilkins-kentucky-basketball-record-debut-steals-rajon-rondo) with seven steals, one shy of the all-time single-game mark, and Pope had previously praised his defensive effort in the Texas Tech exhibition. [Franck Kepnang delivered his best minutes of the preseason or regular season](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/franck-kepnang-showed-valuable-be-bench/) after failing to score in three exhibitions. Milan Momcilovic also [compared freshman Braydon Hawthorne to Kevin Durant](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/braydon-hawthorne-flashed-more-potential-vs-manhattan-reminds-me-of-kevin-durant-a-little-bit/) after Hawthorne showed flashes with a putback dunk and a block.

### Zoom Diallo Responds to Rupp

Zoom Diallo had deactivated his Instagram account before the opener following criticism during the exhibition slate. He finished with 12 points on 4-of-7 shooting with three rebounds, two assists, and two steals, and [thanked the fanbase afterward](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186611/zoom-diallo-thanks-bbn-suppor-uk-debut-joy-out-that-i-needed) for the chants directed his way during the game. Separately, [Mason Williams was spotted knocking down three-point attempts with either hand](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mason-williams-knock-down-3s-either-shooting-hand/) during pregame warmups, having gone 6-of-9 from deep across the three exhibitions before his 7-point, 5-assist debut.

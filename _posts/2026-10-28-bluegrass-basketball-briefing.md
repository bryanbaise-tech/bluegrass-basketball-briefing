## Cats Notebook

### Backcourt Picture After the UCF Exhibition

Kentucky's third exhibition reshaped the point guard debate rather than settling it. [Mason Williams scored 18 points](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-news/185950/uk-basketball-ucf-highlights-box-score-mvp-twitter-reactions-observations) in the 90-76 overtime win, while presumed starter Zoom Diallo has averaged four points and two turnovers across the three preseason games. A Sea of Blue's Drew Holbrook now argues [Williams should start over Diallo](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185982/best-starting-lineup-players), citing superior offensive control and a sub-one turnover rate. Locked On Kentucky reached the [same conclusion in its live reaction](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtwzP9YyBI0), calling Williams the team's best guard right now.

### UCF Game: Warts and a Rescue

Kentucky led by 13 early but needed overtime to put UCF away, outscoring the Knights 14-5 in the extra frame. The Cats went just [9-of-17 from the free throw line in regulation](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185961/missed-free-throws-nearly-cost-kentucky-wildcats-vs-ucf-but-alex-wilkins-saved-the-game), with two late Braydon Hawthorne misses nearly costing them the game before Alex Wilkins hit a floater with under a second left to force overtime. Beyond the free throws, Kentucky [shot 12-of-33 in the second half](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185952/ucf-final-score-recap-4-things-to-know-uk-takeaways) and leaned heavily on perimeter looks rather than attacking the paint. Malachi Moreno posted a [16-point, 13-rebound double-double with eight assists](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-news/185950/uk-basketball-ucf-highlights-box-score-mvp-twitter-reactions-observations), and Milan Momcilovic led all scorers with 19.

### Momcilovic's Shooting Projection

Evan Miyakawa's preseason model has [Momcilovic projected as the top 3-point shooter in college basketball](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185822/advanced-analytics-say-milan-momcilovic-uk-2026-27-season-projections) at 43.4%, up slightly from his 42.8% mark at Iowa State a year ago. He also enters the season with at least 100 more career attempts from deep than any other player in Miyakawa's top seven, giving the projection a larger sample backbone than most names on the list.

### Recruiting: Uvwo Picks Providence

Kentucky missed on its top 2027 frontcourt target. [Five-star center Lewis Uvwo committed to Providence](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/182820/lewis-uvwo-commits-providence-uk-basketball-recruiting-baylor-bears) over Baylor and Cal on Wednesday, choosing Bryan Hodgson's program after visiting Lexington on September 11. Uvwo averaged 6.0 blocks per game at Nike's Peach Jam this summer and ranks as a top-10 player in his class across the three major services. Cameron Waddle had [predicted Baylor as the landing spot](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185903/lewis-uvwo-reese-alston-demarcus-henry-recruiting-predictions-baylor-lsu-unc) and floated Paul Osaruyi and Cherif Millogo as pivot targets for Pope's staff.

### National View

Field of 68's preseason panel framed Kentucky as a team whose [ceiling arrives later in the calendar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e4QWVG_CI9I), suggesting the Cats could be a "monster come January or February" once the new pieces settle. A separate segment pushed back with the opposite take, arguing [Louisville will be meaningfully better than Kentucky](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LwSCQHDI-eQ) this season. The regular season opens Tuesday against Manhattan.

## Cats Notebook

### Rotation Tightens in Exhibition Win

Mark Pope leaned on a short bench in the 76-65 exhibition win over Texas Tech, a departure from the deep-rotation approach that defined last season. [Malachi Moreno logged 35 minutes and Milan Momcilovic added 34](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185299/mark-pope-bench-rotation-texas-tech-uk), with six Wildcats playing 22 minutes or more and the rotation effectively capped at nine. For context, Pope's second exhibition last season against Georgetown saw nine players between 14 and 28 minutes with no one above 30. The early signal suggests Pope views this group as more top-heavy than last year's roster, which featured 10 players averaging over 15 minutes per game.

### Exhibition Standouts

Mason Williams drew attention for his 27-minute run, with [Locked On Kentucky labeling him a standout in the Texas Tech win](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9mdiaSeX5Qs). Ousmane Ndiaye logged 28 minutes and Alex Wilkins 29 in the same game, pointing to the six-man core Pope appears comfortable deploying in competitive stretches.

### Pope on NIL Strategy

Pope indicated Kentucky will not operate as the top bidder in the NIL market, a stance [flagged by Locked On Kentucky as worth unpacking](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=go4cTN9O5tA) given the program's historical recruiting footprint.

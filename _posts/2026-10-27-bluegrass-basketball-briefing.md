## Cats Notebook

### Preseason Positioning

National outlets are converging on Kentucky as a Tier III, second-weekend NCAA Tournament team rather than a top-10 ceiling. Chris Beasmore noted the Wildcats landed [No. 23 in the AP and Coaches polls](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-news/185741/sec-power-rankings-florida-gators-texas-longhorns-tennessee-vols) with questions remaining about defense and depth, while ESPN's Jeff Borzello and Myron Medcalf [slotted Kentucky in Tier III alongside Alabama, Louisville, Michigan and others](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-news/185686/espn-college-basketball-tiers-ranking-uk), framing it as a "make-or-break season for Mark Pope." The Field of 68 crew echoed the second-weekend framing: Rob Dauster [said Kentucky could be "a monster to deal with in SEC play" by late January](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e4QWVG_CI9I&t=0s) once Milan Momcilovic's spacing settles in, though he added the Cats are not in the same conversation as Florida, Texas or Tennessee at the top of the league.

### Exhibition Takeaways

Kentucky's 76-65 win over No. 19 Texas Tech on Friday has recalibrated how the beat views the roster two weeks before the opener. Ethan DeWitt wrote that [Mason Williams was "quite possibly the highest riser"](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185581/uk-stock-report-zoom-diallo-mason-williams) through two exhibitions. The Texas Tech result also doubled as a recruiting backdrop: five-star 2027 combo guard Adan Diggs attended the game during his official visit, and [Cameron Waddle noted Diggs could have taken interest in Wilkins and Williams](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-news/185745/adan-diggs-recruiting-finalists-uk-basketball-arizona-arkansas-usc), both of whom scored in double figures against the Red Raiders.

### 2027 Recruiting Board

The next 10 days could reshape Pope's 2027 class, with three top-10 national prospects in play. Five-star center Lewis Uvwo, [widely regarded as the best shot blocker in the class per Waddle](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185824/lewis-uvwo-college-decision-date-schools-uk-recruiting-predictions-baylor), announces Wednesday between Kentucky, Baylor, California and Providence after a September 11-13 visit to Lexington. No. 2 overall prospect DeMarcus Henry is [down to Kentucky, Arkansas, Louisville and North Carolina with a planned October 30 commitment](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185734/demarcus-henry-college-decision-date-uk-recruiting-north-carolina), and Joe Tipton reported on the Next Gen pod that [the majority of the recent buzz has been around Louisville and Arkansas](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4A1tSxUH1v8&t=0s), with North Carolina still having a window. Diggs, the No. 6 overall senior per Rivals and 247Sports, rounds out the active board alongside USC, Arizona and Arkansas.

### Opponent Context

Auburn's firing of basketball GM Brian Kloman offers a cautionary data point as programs including Kentucky lean harder on front-office hires. Chris Beasmore reported Kloman was dismissed after the school learned he [had not disclosed academic information involving guard Mantas Rubstavicius](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-news/185736/auburn-basketball-fires-gm-brian-kloman-mantas-rubstavicius-eligibility-controversy), who had taken college-level classes in Lithuania before turning pro. Separately, former Wildcat Cason Wallace will miss most or all of the 2026-27 NBA season after Oklahoma City GM Sam Presti announced [Wallace suffered a torn labrum requiring surgery](https://www.aseaofblue.com/former-kentucky-wildcats/185700/cason-wallace-injury-surgery-recovery-timeline-oklahoma-city-thunder) less than a week into his new extension.

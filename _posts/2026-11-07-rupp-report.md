Kentucky meets [Kansas on Tuesday night at the United Center in Chicago](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401909786/kansas-kentucky) in the Champions Classic, with tip at 9:30 PM ET on ESPN. The Jayhawks arrive [ranked second nationally with an elite defense, a high-powered offense, and an up-tempo profile per Bart Torvik](https://barttorvik.com/team.php?team=Kansas&year=2027), a combination that makes them one of the toughest early-season tests on the schedule. Torvik's ratings-based projection pegs Kentucky as roughly a seven-point underdog. [A Sea of Blue notes](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186983/kam-williams-returns-injury-james-madison-uk-kansas) that Kam Williams returned from a lengthy foot-surgery absence against James Madison, giving Mark Pope another backcourt option heading into Chicago.

## Cats Notebook

### Guards set the tone in the JMU survival

Kentucky's backcourt carried the offense in the [89-80 win over James Madison](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-backcourt-trio-didnt-log-single-turnover-vs-james-madison/), with Zoom Diallo, Alex Wilkins, and Mason Williams combining for 77 minutes and zero turnovers. [Diallo posted his best game as a Wildcat](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/zoom-diallo-and-justin-mcbride-discuss-their-big-nights-in-kentuckys-win-over-jmu/) with 16 points on 7-of-11 shooting in 22 minutes, getting downhill and finishing at the rim. Mark Pope [credited the fan response to Diallo](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-popes-postgame-comments-on-bbn-zoom-diallo-justin-mcbride-kam-williams-and-more/) for lifting the point guard, and Diallo returned the sentiment by calling Rupp Arena one of the loudest buildings in the country. Wilkins added 16 points, while Williams chipped in 12 points and five assists off the bench.

### Justin McBride's reunion with Preston Spradlin

McBride delivered 13 points and a team-high eight rebounds against his former program, shooting 3-of-4 from three in 27 minutes off the bench. [Spradlin joked afterward](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/justin-mcbride-came-up-huge-kentucky-against-former-team/) that he wished McBride had shot that well playing for him, but praised the defensive strides his former player has made. Pope said the staff spent months [texting McBride a single word, "rebound,"](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-popes-postgame-comments-on-bbn-zoom-diallo-justin-mcbride-kam-williams-and-more/) and the point stuck on Friday.

### Kam Williams returns on a minutes limit

Sophomore Kam Williams made his season debut after seven months away from multiple foot surgeries, playing nine minutes and scoring two points at the free-throw line. Pope [confirmed he was on a 12-minute cap](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kam-williams-is-back-and-mark-pope-expects-him-to-put-his-fingerprints-on-kentucky/) and said the priority was dipping Williams back into a competitive environment rather than production. Pope expects a larger role against Kansas in the Champions Classic, calling Williams a player who "makes some really hard things look easy."

### Defense remains the open question

The win masked real concerns on the defensive end, where James Madison attempted 45 three-pointers and made 15. [KSR's Jack Pilgrim noted the defense took a clear step back](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/ksrs-takeaways-from-kentuckys-too-close-for-comfort-win-over-jmu/) from the preseason baseline that had been ahead of the offense. Issues Pope will have to address before Tuesday's matchup with Kansas in Chicago.

### Pointing toward Kansas

Diallo [called the Champions Classic game a "must-win"](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-popes-postgame-comments-on-bbn-zoom-diallo-justin-mcbride-kam-williams-and-more/) and a statement opportunity for where the program sits nationally. Kentucky will face a Kansas roster that now includes Trey Kaufman-Renn, Melvin Council, and Tre White as fifth-year additions, giving Bill Self a frontcourt and depth upgrade that [Jeff Goodman described as pushing the Jayhawks back into national-title contention](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2ReQIBFX2AY&t=2820s) on Field of 68.

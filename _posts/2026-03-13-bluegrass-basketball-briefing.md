### Game Analysis

Florida pulled away in the final two minutes after Kentucky had clawed back within striking distance. Denzel Aberdeen's 25-foot triple with 1:21 left cut the deficit to 66-61 and lifted Kentucky's win probability to 9.7%, but Xaivian Lee answered with a 25-footer at the 0:50 mark that pushed the Gators ahead 69-61 and effectively closed the door, dropping Kentucky to a 2.7% win probability *([ESPN game center](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401851565), 2026-03-13)*. Thomas Haugh's free throw with 2:35 to play had already tilted the leverage, spiking Florida's win probability to 93.9% *([ESPN game center](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401851565), 2026-03-13)*.

The hole was dug in the first half. Florida outscored Kentucky 37-28 over the opening 20 minutes, and a Boogie Fland mid-range jumper at the 8:56 mark stretched the lead in a sequence that swung win probability four-and-a-half points toward the Gators *([ESPN game center](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401851565), 2026-03-13)*. Kentucky's 35-point second half got the margin back to single digits but never produced a lead change.

The four factors tell the story cleanly. Kentucky shot a .398 eFG% against Florida's .405, a near wash, but the Gators won the possession battle decisively with a 43.2 offensive rebound rate and a .414 free throw rate, against Kentucky's 21.6% OREB and .271 FTR *([Sports Reference](https://www.sports-reference.com/cbb/boxscores/2026-03-13-13-florida.html), 2026-03-13)*. Kentucky did force turnovers on 17.8% of Florida possessions while coughing it up on only 13.8% of its own, but the extra trips Florida generated on the glass and at the line erased that edge *([Sports Reference](https://www.sports-reference.com/cbb/boxscores/2026-03-13-13-florida.html), 2026-03-13)*.

Aberdeen led Kentucky with 17 points on 5-of-11 shooting, 2-of-5 from three, and a 61.4 true shooting percentage in 35 minutes *([Sports Reference](https://www.sports-reference.com/cbb/boxscores/2026-03-13-13-florida.html), 2026-03-13)*. Mouhamed Dioubate provided the most efficient line off the bench at 14 points on 5-of-7 shooting and an 83.1 TS%, though he fouled out *([Sports Reference](https://www.sports-reference.com/cbb/boxscores/2026-03-13-13-florida.html), 2026-03-13)*. Otega Oweh carried a team-high 28.6% usage but finished 5-of-18 for 10 points on a 27.8 TS% with four turnovers, the central drag on the offense *([Sports Reference](https://www.sports-reference.com/cbb/boxscores/2026-03-13-13-florida.html), 2026-03-13)*. Foul trouble compounded the issue, with Chandler and Dioubate both fouling out and Garrison picking up four.

Kentucky entered with a Torvik profile of 120.0 adjusted offense and 100.1 adjusted defense for a .8888 Barthag *([Bart Torvik](https://www.barttorvik.com/team.php?team=Kentucky&year=2026), 2026-03-13)*, and the offense fell well short of that baseline at 63 points on the kind of shot volume Florida's rebounding allowed. The nine assists against 12 turnovers, paired with a 36.0 FG%, points to a half-court attack that never found rhythm against the Gators' size *([Sports Reference](https://www.sports-reference.com/cbb/boxscores/2026-03-13-13-florida.html), 2026-03-13)*.

## Cats Notebook

### NBA Combine and the Moreno Decision

Malachi Moreno measured 6'11.25" barefoot at 242.8 pounds with a 7'1.5" wingspan and a 9'3.5" standing reach at the NBA Draft Combine in Chicago *([Kentucky Sports Radio](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/see-how-malachi-moreno-measured-in-at-the-nba-draft-combine/), 2026-05-12)*. He subsequently withdrew from the five-on-five scrimmages, with The Ringer's Kevin O'Connor reporting Moreno has "garnered intrigue from teams in the mid-late first round range" *([Kentucky Sports Radio](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/malachi-moreno-will-not-scrimmage-at-the-nba-draft-combine-after-all/), 2026-05-12)*. O'Connor's post-lottery mock has Moreno going No. 39 to Houston, though his big board ranks the freshman 30th, calling him "one of the clear risers in the pre-draft process" *([Kentucky Sports Radio](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/malachi-moreno-will-not-scrimmage-at-the-nba-draft-combine-after-all/), 2026-05-12)*. A Sea of Blue characterized skipping scrimmages as a signal Moreno likes his current stock, framing a stay-in-draft outcome as a "brutal development" for a roster built around him at the five *([A Sea of Blue](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172161/malachi-moreno-nba-draft-combine-scrimmages-first-round-pick), 2026-05-11)*. Jayden Quaintance also measured at the combine, coming in at 6'9" barefoot and 253 pounds with a 7'5.25" wingspan and a 9'1" standing reach, dimensions Jonathan Givony called "extremely impressive" for an NBA big man *([Kentucky Sports Radio](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/jayden-quaintance-posts-impressive-measurements-at-nba-draft-combine/), 2026-05-11)*.

### Frontcourt Insurance: Kepnang Cleared

Washington transfer Franck Kepnang received a medical hardship waiver granting him a seventh and final season of eligibility, the school confirmed *([Kentucky Sports Radio](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/franck-kepnang-receives-waiver-to-play-for-kentucky-basketball-2026-27/), 2026-05-11)*. The 6'11", 253-pound center averaged 6.2 points, 6.3 rebounds, and 2.1 blocks per game last season while shooting 51.5% from the field, appearing in 27 games with 25 starts *([A Sea of Blue](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172156/franck-kepnang-eligible-for-kentucky-for-seventh-and-final-season), 2026-05-11)*. His career has been marked by extended injury absences at Washington, including an ACL injury that ended his first season after eight games and limited stints of 10 and 14 games in subsequent years *([Kentucky Sports Radio](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/franck-kepnang-receives-waiver-to-play-for-kentucky-basketball-2026-27/), 2026-05-11)*. The addition also reunites Kepnang with Zoom Diallo, his Washington teammate last season *([Kentucky Sports Radio](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/franck-kepnang-receives-waiver-to-play-for-kentucky-basketball-2026-27/), 2026-05-11)*.

### N'Diaye Scouting Report

Wiltjer emphasized the level of competition N'Diaye has faced overseas, noting the league features "a lot of grown men" with high-level professional experience *([Kentucky Sports Radio](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kyle-wiltjer-says-fans-should-be-excited-about-ousmane-ndiaye-and-the-future-of-kentucky-basketball/), 2026-05-12)*.

### Pope's Q&A Series and Front Office Build

Mark Pope wrapped up a four-part offseason Q&A series with Big Blue Nation, addressing his push for 3-point shooting, agent relationships, and recruiting visits, including a story about ending a portal visit after three hours *([Kentucky Sports Radio](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-says-he-ended-a-portal-visit-after-three-hours-in-final-offseason-bbn-qa/), 2026-05-12)*. The series followed criticism of Pope for staying quiet during a polarizing offseason that included losing top 2026 prospect Tyran Stokes to Kansas *([Kentucky Sports Radio](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-says-he-ended-a-portal-visit-after-three-hours-in-final-offseason-bbn-qa/), 2026-05-12)*. Brown was hired in late March after previously working with Pope at BYU and most recently serving as a video coordinator for the G-League's Wisconsin Herd *([A Sea of Blue](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172032/keegan-brown-role-job), 2026-05-12)*.

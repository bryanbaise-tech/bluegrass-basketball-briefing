### Game Analysis

Florida closed the door inside the final minute when [Xaivian Lee's 25-foot three with 50 seconds left](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401851565) pushed the Gators' win probability to 97.3% and stretched the lead to 69-61. Kentucky had clawed within striking distance moments earlier, as [Denzel Aberdeen's 25-foot three at 1:21](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401851565) cut the margin to five and lifted UK's win probability to 9.7%, the high-water mark of the closing stretch.

The first half set the terms. Florida built a 37-28 cushion behind early scoring punches, including [Lee's opening 25-foot three at the 17:42 mark](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401851565) and [Boogie Fland's 16-foot jumper with 8:56 left](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401851565) that pushed the Gators' win probability above 80%. Kentucky's lone first-half counter of note came when [Mouhamed Dioubate hit a 25-foot three off a Jasper Johnson feed](https://www.espn.com/mens-college-basketball/game/_/gameId/401851565), trimming the deficit to 20-18 and nudging UK's win probability to 21.5%.

The four factors tell the structural story. Florida [crushed the offensive glass at 43.2% versus Kentucky's 21.6%](https://www.sports-reference.com/cbb/boxscores/2026-03-13-13-florida.html) and got to the line at a [.414 free throw rate against UK's .271](https://www.sports-reference.com/cbb/boxscores/2026-03-13-13-florida.html). The shooting numbers were nearly identical, with Florida at a .405 eFG% to Kentucky's .398, but the possession and free throw gaps proved decisive. UK's 17.8% turnover rate forced by the defense was a positive, just not enough to overcome the rebounding margin.

[Aberdeen led Kentucky with 17 points on 61.4% true shooting](https://www.sports-reference.com/cbb/boxscores/2026-03-13-13-florida.html), hitting 5-of-11 from the field and 2-of-5 from three while adding five rebounds and three assists in 35 minutes. [Dioubate provided the most efficient line of the night at 83.1% TS on 5-of-7 shooting for 14 points](https://www.sports-reference.com/cbb/boxscores/2026-03-13-13-florida.html), though he fouled out. [Otega Oweh struggled to a 27.8% TS on 5-of-18 shooting](https://www.sports-reference.com/cbb/boxscores/2026-03-13-13-florida.html) despite a team-high 28.6% usage, and the perimeter went 0-for-3 from deep with four turnovers across his 39 minutes. [Andrija Jelavic's 16.7% TS on 1-of-6](https://www.sports-reference.com/cbb/boxscores/2026-03-13-13-florida.html) compounded the shot-quality problems for a Kentucky team that finished 36% from the floor.

Context from the season profile cuts both ways for what comes next. Kentucky entered with a [120.0 AdjOE and .8888 Barthag per Bart Torvik](https://www.barttorvik.com/team.php?team=Kentucky&year=2026). The 100.1 AdjDE remains the area where Saturday's offensive rebounding breakdown will draw the most scrutiny heading into Selection Sunday.

## Cats Notebook

### NBA Draft Combine Watch

Malachi Moreno's status hovers over the rest of the offseason. He measured 6-11.25 barefoot at 242.8 pounds with a 7-1.5 wingspan and 9-3.5 standing reach in [Chicago](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/see-how-malachi-moreno-measured-in-at-the-nba-draft-combine/), then [pulled out of the five-on-five scrimmages](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/malachi-moreno-will-not-scrimmage-at-the-nba-draft-combine-after-all/) after Kevin O'Connor reported teams in the mid-to-late first round have shown interest. Kevin O'Connor now says Moreno has a [pretty strong chance of going in the first round](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172161/malachi-moreno-nba-draft-combine-scrimmages-first-round-pick), which Jason Marcum frames as a brutal development for a rebuilt roster banking on the Great Crossing product at the 5. Wildcat Blue Nation reports the rest of the [roster construction is effectively frozen](https://wildcatbluenation.com/kentucky-s-roster-is-on-hold-until-malachi-moreno-makes-his-nba-draft-choice-01krbz4frbv6?utm_source=RSS) until he decides. Former teammate Jayden Quaintance also measured well, coming in at 6-9 barefoot and 253 pounds with a [7-5.25 wingspan and 9-1 standing reach](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/jayden-quaintance-posts-impressive-measurements-at-nba-draft-combine/).

### Frontcourt Insurance

Kentucky added a hedge against Moreno's departure when [Franck Kepnang received a medical hardship waiver](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/franck-kepnang-receives-waiver-to-play-for-kentucky-basketball-2026-27/) for a seventh and final college season. The 6-11 Washington transfer averaged [6.2 points, 6.3 rebounds, and 2.1 blocks](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172156/franck-kepnang-eligible-for-kentucky-for-seventh-and-final-season) on 51.5% shooting last season, though his career has been shaped by repeated injuries, including an ACL tear and multiple shortened seasons in Seattle. The other potential frontcourt addition looks less likely: Wildcat Blue Nation flagged an [ESPN update on Milan Momcilovic](https://wildcatbluenation.com/milan-momcilovic-update-espn-insider-provides-news-kentucky-fans-didn-t-want-to-hear?utm_source=RSS) that fans did not want to hear, even as the Iowa State sharpshooter's measurements (6-8 barefoot, 218.2 pounds) were [logged at the combine](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/ksr-today-all-eyes-on-nba-draft-combine-malachi-moreno-milan-momcilovic/).

### Backcourt and New Faces

Assistant coach Mo Williams went on record with high expectations for the new guards he helped recruit. [Williams predicted Zoom Diallo will land on one of the three All-SEC teams](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mo-williams-predicts-big-things-from-zoom-diallo-alex-wilkins-kentucky/), pointing to his two seasons at Washington (15.7 points, 4.5 assists as a sophomore) and his readiness for the level. Former Wildcat Kyle Wiltjer, who faced new addition Ousmane N'Diaye twice in Italy's Serie A, told Tom Leach that the 6-11 Senegalese forward [shoots it well, is super athletic defensively, and finishes strong at the rim](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kyle-wiltjer-says-fans-should-be-excited-about-ousmane-ndiaye-and-the-future-of-kentucky-basketball/). Brandon Ramsey's film breakdown of James Madison transfer [Justin McBride](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/film-room-justin-mcbride/) notes the Third Team All-Sun Belt forward's commitment was overshadowed by landing roughly an hour before Tyran Stokes picked Kansas, which Ramsey calls a misguided rollout that did real damage to the public reaction.

### Pope's Q&A and Front Office

Mark Pope closed his offseason fan Q&A series with a fourth installment, where he [described ending a portal visit after roughly three hours](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-says-he-ended-a-portal-visit-after-three-hours-in-final-offseason-bbn-qa/) when the fit wasn't right, alongside answers on 3-point shooting emphasis, agent relationships, and recruiting visits. The behind-the-scenes work supporting those decisions falls in part to Keegan Brown, who [described his role on social media](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/172032/keegan-brown-role-job) as managing contracts start to finish, building models with Nick Robinson, and talking to agents daily, with analytics serving as the foundation rather than the full job description.

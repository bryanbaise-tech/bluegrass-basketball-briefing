## Cats Notebook

### Fallout from the Champions Classic

The national and local reaction to Kentucky's 89-69 loss to Kansas has been uniformly brutal. [John Fanta called the Mark Pope era "a disappointment"](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/john-fanta-the-mark-pope-era-has-been-a-disappointment/) on The EvanMiya College Basketball Show, with Evan Miyakawa adding that watching the game felt like Kansas was playing a West Virginia, Oklahoma State, or Cincinnati in the Big 12 Tournament rather than a blue blood. [Tristan Pharis at A Sea of Blue noted Pope has now lost five games by 20 or more points in just 75 games at Kentucky](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/187728/mark-pope-kentucky-basketball-history-20-point-losses), matching John Calipari's total in 533 games and surpassing Rick Pitino's four in 269. Former Wildcat [Aaron Harrison offered a counter-message on Twitter](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/187676/aaron-harrison-encouraging-message-kentucky), telling the current team to stick together, tune out the media, and figure it out.

### Diagnosing What Went Wrong

The beat is converging on offense-first concerns tied to Milan Momcilovic's non-involvement and Zoom Diallo's ball-dominance. Momcilovic finished with five points on seven shots and attempted zero three-pointers in the first half. Diallo's 24 points on 15 shots with zero assists and two turnovers drew concern on [Sources Say, where Jack Pilgrim and Spencer Smith called it unsustainable](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dq_TbUNwXsM&t=360s) and noted Kansas appeared content to let Diallo hunt his own offense. The Field of 68 crew was [similarly baffled by the lack of offensive flow](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YBec-W9Cipg&t=0s), with the panel contrasting it unfavorably against Pope's old BYU offenses that coaches around the country used to copy.

### Front Court and Lineup Questions

Malachi Moreno's two early fouls, including what Smith called a "ghost" second foul, forced Pope to extended minutes to Frank Kepnang and three first-half minutes for Reece Potter during the stretch Kansas built its cushion. Smith and Pilgrim both advocated for more minutes with Momcilovic sliding to the four alongside Braden Hawthorne at the three, noting Hawthorne's defensive moment on Tyran Stokes and a step-through finish as evidence the freshman deserves a longer look. Andrija Jelavic committed four fouls in 12 minutes and Justin McBride went 0-for-3 in 14 minutes.

### Pregame Access Cut for Northern Arizona

Pope will not hold a pregame press conference ahead of Friday's game against Northern Arizona, which [Tyler Thompson and Drew Franklin flagged on KSR Happy Hour](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=19ZdkHFmL5Q&t=2700s) as a departure from standard practice and part of a broader access reduction this season that has also eliminated midweek player availabilities. Franklin pointed out that Diallo's 24-point performance was the exact kind of story the program could have leaned into with a pregame podium appearance. [Northern Arizona enters at 0-3](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/187690/northern-arizona-time-tv-channel-online-stream-predictions-odds), with losses to UNLV, New Mexico, and Arizona.

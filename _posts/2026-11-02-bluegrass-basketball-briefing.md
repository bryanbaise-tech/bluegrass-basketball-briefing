## Cats Notebook

### Season Tips With Manhattan at Rupp

Kentucky opens Mark Pope's third season Tuesday at 7 p.m. ET against Manhattan, with [KenPom giving the Wildcats a 99.5 percent chance to win and projecting a 94-65 final](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-opens-as-overwhelming-favorite-in-opener-vs-manhattan/). The [Jaspers are picked 10th of 12 in the Metro preseason poll](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-opens-as-overwhelming-favorite-in-opener-vs-manhattan/) and return just three players with meaningful experience from a 12-20 team. [Nick Roush noted Kentucky is 102-21 all-time in season openers and 46-4 in home openers at Rupp](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/how-kentucky-basketball-has-fared-in-season-openers/), with Pope sitting 2-0 in openers at UK.

### Momcilovic Carrying the Preseason Hype

Milan Momcilovic enters the opener with accolade after accolade stacking up, most recently a [Naismith Trophy preseason watch list selection](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/milan-momcilovic-naismith-trophy-preseason-watch-list/) to go with preseason All-American nods from CBS Sports, 247 Sports and Sports Illustrated. The Iowa State transfer led Kentucky's three exhibitions in scoring at 16.7 points per game while shooting 9-of-20 from three. Pope wants more volume, however. Momcilovic [averaged 10.3 field goal attempts and 6.7 threes per exhibition, down from 11.1 shots and 7.5 three-point attempts per game at Iowa State last season](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/milan-momcilovic-led-kentucky-shots-during-preseason-but-mark-pope-wants-even-more-looks/), and the coaching staff wants those numbers to climb once the regular season begins.

### Recruiting: Millogo Trending, Hampton Holds

Pope's 2027 recruiting board got clearer on two fronts Monday. [Joe Tipton reported Mark Pope and Kentucky held their lead for 7-foot-5 center Cherif Millogo](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186371/cherif-millogo-byu-usc-visits-paul-osaruyi-uk-recruiting) even after the St. Francis (CA) prospect took weekend visits to BYU and USC, with Tipton writing he would be surprised to see Millogo land anywhere other than Kentucky. Millogo also [jumped from No. 30 to No. 11 overall in the updated Rivals150, becoming the No. 2 center in the class](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/where-ryan-hampton-cherif-millogo-landed-in-new-2027-rivals150-rankings/). Pledge Ryan Hampton slid out of the consensus top 10 but [checks in at No. 27 overall and No. 3 at shooting guard](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/where-ryan-hampton-cherif-millogo-landed-in-new-2027-rivals150-rankings/), trailing only Malachi Moreno among Pope's highest-ranked career commits.

## Cats Notebook

### Preseason Recognition

Kentucky's two headliners drew national hardware nods as the regular season approached. [Milan Momcilovic](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/milan-momcilovic-clara-strack-national-player-year-candidates/) landed on the 20-man NABC Player of the Year preseason watch list, while Clara Strack made the 50-player Naismith Trophy women's watch list. The SEC coaches followed [the media's lead on preseason All-SEC teams](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/milan-momcilovic-and-malachi-moreno-named-to-coaches-preseason-all-sec-teams/), slotting Momcilovic on the second team and Malachi Moreno on the third. Momcilovic led the country last season in both total three-pointers made (136) and three-point percentage (48.7%) at Iowa State, and he backed up the hype in exhibitions by averaging [16.7 points on 45 percent three-point shooting](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/milan-momcilovic-lived-up-hype-preseason/) across three games.

### Point Guard Rotation

The Zoom Diallo adjustment is the central rotation story heading into the opener. Pope kept Diallo on the bench for key second-half and overtime stretches in both high-major exhibitions, and [Diallo deactivated his Instagram account](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-isnt-worried-about-zoom-diallo-im-telling-you-hes-gonna-have-big-moments-for-us/) after the UCF game amid fan criticism, though he still tied for the team lead with 14 preseason assists. Pope's public message is patience, telling reporters Diallo is learning to get the ball out of his hands quicker than he did at Washington and predicting "he's gonna have big moments for us." Freshman Mason Williams has complicated the depth chart by averaging 14 points on 69.6 percent shooting and 66.7 percent from three across the three exhibitions, with [Pope and UCF's Johnny Dawkins both describing him](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mason-williams-continues-to-impress-hes-going-to-be-a-terrific-terrific-player-he-already-is/) as a "terrific player." A Sea of Blue's Dustin Holbrook went further, arguing [Williams should be starting over Diallo](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/185982/best-starting-lineup-players) based on the preseason gap in production and turnovers.

### Frontcourt Development

Moreno's closing performance against UCF was the single data point Pope pointed to when asked what he learned about his team. The sophomore center [posted 16 points, 14 rebounds, eight assists and two blocks in 37 minutes](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/malachi-moreno-nearly-messed-around-and-got-a-triple-double/) with only two turnovers, and Pope singled out the four-to-one assist-to-turnover ratio as the reason for optimism. Freshman Braydon Hawthorne, who did not play a minute last season, [added seven points, three rebounds, four blocks and two steals in 18 minutes](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/malachi-moreno-says-braydon-hawthorne-will-be-the-story-of-this-season-for-kentucky/) against the Knights, with Moreno predicting Hawthorne "will be the story of this season" for Kentucky.

### Injury Report

Kam Williams is close but not cleared. Pope described Williams as [essentially a two-time Jones fracture case](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mark-pope-provides-injury-update-on-kam-williams-hes-going-to-give-us-another-huge-boost/), with the original injury in January followed by a spring setback that cost him the summer and preseason. The junior wing has moved to 5-on-0 work in practice and will get live reps against the scout team on Friday, with Pope saying Williams "will be incredibly cautious" but is on track to return during the regular season.

### Recruiting Board

The 2027 point guard board took a hit when five-star Reese Alston [committed to LSU](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/5-star-pg-reese-alston-commits-to-lsu-2/) over Kentucky, Louisville, Tennessee and Wichita State. Alston, ranked No. 9 overall and No. 3 at his position, took his official visit to Lexington on September 29, but the Rivals Prediction Machine had LSU at 90.7 percent entering his announcement. The board also lost five-star center Lewis Uvwo, who [picked Providence over Baylor and Kentucky](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/182820/lewis-uvwo-commits-providence-uk-basketball-recruiting-baylor-bears) after visiting Lexington on September 11.

### National Perspective

The outside read on Kentucky is cautiously positive. UCF's Johnny Dawkins said after the exhibition that [Kentucky is a "terrific team"](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-a-terrific-team-with-a-lot-of-potential-says-johnny-dawkins/) and will "become a great team" by year's end, pointing to the closing stretch when the Wildcats finished with 23 assists on 32 made field goals against just 12 turnovers. The exhibitions showed real perimeter defensive concerns: UCF nearly stole the game before Alex Wilkins forced overtime with a runner at the buzzer.

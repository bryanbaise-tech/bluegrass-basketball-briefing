## Cats Notebook

### Recruiting Pivot

Kentucky's 2027 class took shape in a dramatic 48 hours. Mark Pope landed 7-foot-5 center [Cherif Millogo](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/7-foot-5-center-cherif-millogo-commits-to-kentucky-2/), the No. 11 overall recruit, who chose UK over BYU, USC, Arkansas, Louisville, North Carolina, Tennessee, Arizona and Texas. Within a day the staff then [pulled out of recruitments](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-no-longer-pursuing-aj-williams-adan-diggs/) for five-stars AJ Williams and Adan Diggs, canceling Williams' scheduled official visit for this weekend and ending pursuit of Diggs before his November 16 commitment. [A Sea of Blue noted](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186812/aj-williams-adan-diggs-recruit-uk-byu-arizona) that CJ Rosser is essentially the lone remaining uncommitted 2027 target with mutual interest, with 2028 reclass candidates Yann Kamagate and Benji Bourret still in the picture. Pope also [briefly inquired on Robbie Avila](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186814/robbie-avila-saint-louis-missouri-uk-recruiting-transfers) before the Saint Louis guard secured an injunction to return there.

### JMU and Coach Pope's Connection

James Madison visits Rupp Friday with a familiar face on the opposing sideline. Dukes coach [Preston Spradlin](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/preston-spradlin-joins-sources-say-to-talk-kentucky-vs-jmu/) served on John Calipari's Kentucky staff from 2009 to 2014 and joined KSR's Sources Say to discuss the matchup, his relationship with Pope, and how the game came together. JMU enters as [the No. 163 team](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186701/james-madison-time-tv-channel-online-stream-odds-predictions) after going 18-15 last year and finishing eighth in the Sun Belt in Spradlin's second season. Kentucky uses the game as a tune-up before facing Kansas on November 10 in Chicago.

### Early-Season Returns

The opening-night film gave Pope two things to build on. [Milan Momcilovic](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-basketball/186556/kentucky-basketball-3-point-shooting-win-manhattan) went 6-of-9 from three against Manhattan, tying Antonio Reeves for the most threes by a Wildcat in a regular-season debut, as Kentucky finished 12-of-31 from deep. Pope has publicly targeted 30 to 35 three-point attempts per game, and the opener hit that mark. Defensively, [Zac Oakes noted](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-news/186779/uk-basketball-defensive-potential) the Cats produced nine steals and four blocks, with Pope saying length has to be this roster's defensive calling card. Freshman [Mason Williams](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/mason-williams-knock-down-3s-either-shooting-hand/) added seven points and five assists in 19 minutes and drew attention in warmups for knocking down threes with either hand.

### National Perception

Outside evaluators are slightly higher on Kentucky than the preseason polls. [Jay Bilas slotted the Wildcats at No. 19](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/jay-bilas-believes-kentucky-is-top-20-team-in-college-basketball/) in his Bilas Index and sixth in the SEC, compared to No. 23 in the AP and Coaches polls and a projected seventh-place SEC finish. On the individual side, ESPN's Jeremy Woo [ranked Malachi Moreno No. 14](https://on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/malachi-moreno-viewed-potential-lottery-pick-2027-nba-draft/) in his updated top 100 for the 2027 NBA Draft, placing the returning sophomore in lottery territory after he averaged 7.8 points and 6.3 rebounds in 22.6 minutes as a freshman.

## Recruiting and Portal

### Targets

- **[AJ Williams](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-no-longer-pursuing-aj-williams-adan-diggs)** — Kentucky has pulled out of the recruitment of the five-star small forward and No. 5 overall prospect in 2027, canceling an official visit that had been scheduled for Lexington this weekend.
- **[Adan Diggs](https://www.on3.com/rivals/news/5-star-sg-adan-diggs-sets-commitment-date-arkansas-arizona-kentucky-usc)** — The five-star combo guard set a November 16 commitment date with Arkansas, Arizona, Kentucky, and USC as finalists, though [Kentucky has since withdrawn from his recruitment](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-no-longer-pursuing-aj-williams-adan-diggs) with Arizona viewed as the overwhelming favorite.
- **[CJ Rosser](https://www.on3.com/teams/kentucky-wildcats/news/kentucky-no-longer-pursuing-aj-williams-adan-diggs)** — With UK out on Williams and Diggs, the No. 2 overall prospect is described as essentially the only remaining uncommitted 2027 target the Wildcats are actively pursuing; Rosser has publicly stated he plans to commit in January or February.
- **[Nasir Anderson](https://www.si.com/college/tennessee/recruiting/tennessee-basketball-reportedly-a-leader-in-5-star-s-recruitment-01m6mbw8sh64)** — Rivals' Joe Tipton reports Tennessee and Virginia have emerged as the two leaders for the five-star point guard, who recently visited Tennessee, Virginia, Georgia, UNC, and Providence.
